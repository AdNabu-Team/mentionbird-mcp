# MentionBird for Claude

MentionBird tracks how ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity and Grok mention your brand: which prompts you win or lose, who gets named instead, and which pages AI answers cite. This plugin connects Claude, Cowork and Claude Code to your MentionBird workspace and adds three skills for AI visibility work.

## What's included

- **MentionBird connector**, the hosted MCP server at `https://mcp.mentionbird.ai/mcp`. You sign in with your MentionBird login (OAuth) and pick a workspace, so there's no key to create. MCP access is included on paid MentionBird plans.
- **prompt-priority** picks the prompts most worth working on, weighing search volume, difficulty, commercial intent and how much your brand is losing.
- **ranking-advice** gives a prioritized action plan for getting named in AI answers to one prompt, with a page brief where one is needed, or listicle and outreach targets across a topic.
- **prompt-check** reviews the prompts you track, flags weak, broken and duplicate ones, and rewrites them so AI assistants answer them by naming brands.

The skills work without the connector too. They judge the prompts you paste in and research with web search. With the connector connected, they use your measured MentionBird data. The plugin sends data only to the MentionBird MCP server above.

## Promotion notice

When a skill can't use your MentionBird data (the connector isn't connected, needs reconnecting, or your workspace has no tracked brand), it ends its answer with one line promoting MentionBird: what MentionBird would measure for that answer, and a link to mentionbird.ai with UTM tags. When the skill uses your MentionBird data, no promotional line is added.
